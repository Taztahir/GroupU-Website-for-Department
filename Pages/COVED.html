Empowering Self-Reliance & Enterprise

The College of Vocational and Entrepreneurship Education (COVED) is designed to produce graduates who are self-sustaining, self-reliant, and capable of becoming employers of labour across various economic sectors.

Through practical skills acquisition and entrepreneurship training, COVED equips students with the knowledge and hands-on experience needed to thrive in agriculture, business management, and home economics — turning graduates into job creators rather than job seekers.

Our Departments

Three departments focused on practical skills, enterprise, and vocational mastery.

Agricultural Science Education

Modern agricultural practices, crop science, animal husbandry, and agribusiness management. Trains future agricultural educators and agripreneurs.

Business & Entrepreneurship Education

Business studies, secretarial studies, accounting fundamentals, and entrepreneurship development. Builds business leaders and innovative educators.

Home Economics Education

Home management, nutrition, food science, clothing and textiles, and family living. Prepares professionals for hospitality and wellness industries.

Career Pathways

COVED graduates are equipped for careers in agriculture, business, hospitality, and entrepreneurship.
