Our Story

Lagos State University of Education

Lagos State University of Education (LASUED) was established by the Lagos State Government to address the growing demand for qualified teachers and education professionals in Nigeria. The university was carved out of the former Adeniran Ogunsanya College of Education (AOCOED) and granted full university status.

LASUED is structured into 8 specialised colleges offering B.Sc., B.A., and B.Ed. degree programmes that produce competent educators, researchers, and professionals equipped to transform Nigeria's education landscape and beyond.

Located along the Badagry Expressway in Oto/Ijanikin, Lagos State, the university serves as a hub for academic excellence, innovative research, and community development.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To produce highly skilled, competent, and morally upright graduates through quality teaching, research, and community service. LASUED is committed to training educators who will drive innovation, foster critical thinking, and advance knowledge across all disciplines.

Our Vision

To be a world-class university of education, recognised for excellence in teacher training, academic research, and the holistic development of individuals who will positively impact society and drive sustainable national development.
