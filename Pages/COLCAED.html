Mastering Language & The Power of Communication

The College of Languages and Communication Arts Education (COLCAED) focuses on language acquisition, literature, and communication studies. Students develop mastery in English, foreign languages like Arabic and French, Nigerian indigenous languages, and modern communication arts.

COLCAED graduates are versatile professionals who excel as language educators, media practitioners, translators, public relations specialists, and diplomatic service officers — bridging cultures and shaping narratives across the globe.

Our Departments

Four departments covering English, foreign languages, Nigerian languages, and communication arts.

English Language

English literature, grammar, phonetics, creative writing, and applied linguistics. Prepares skilled English educators and literary professionals.

Foreign Languages Education

Arabic and French language studies, cross-cultural communication, and translation. Opens doors to international careers and diplomatic services.

Nigerian Languages Education

Yoruba, Igbo, and other indigenous language studies, cultural preservation, and mother-tongue education pedagogy.

Language Arts & Communication

Mass communication, journalism, public speaking, media studies, and digital communication strategies for the modern world.
