Cultivating Culture, Creativity & Critical Thought

The College of Humanities Education (COHED) is dedicated to the study of human culture, creative expression, and moral values. Students explore arts, history, religion, and performance — developing cultural literacy essential for educators and creative professionals.

COHED prepares graduates who can teach, inspire, and lead in fields ranging from fine arts and music to international diplomacy and cultural heritage preservation.

Our Departments

Five departments fostering creativity, cultural awareness, and historical understanding.

Fine Arts Education

Visual arts, painting, sculpture, graphic design, and art history. Prepares creative educators and professional artists.

History & Diplomatic Studies

Nigerian and world history, international relations, and diplomacy. Builds analytical skills for governance and scholarship.

Music Education

Music theory, performance, composition, and ethnomusicology. Trains music educators and professional performers.

Religions & Peace Studies

Christian Religious Studies, Islamic Studies, and peace-building. Explores moral philosophy, ethics, and interfaith dialogue.

Theatre Arts

Acting, directing, stagecraft, film production, and dramatic literature. Prepares performers and media professionals.
