Building Business Leaders & Financial Experts

The College of Management Science Education (COMSED) produces future-ready professionals in accounting, finance, and office administration. Students develop strong analytical, organisational, and management skills that are highly sought after in both the public and private sectors.

COMSED graduates are equipped not only to teach management and business subjects but also to excel as accountants, auditors, financial analysts, office managers, and entrepreneurs — making them versatile assets in Nigeria's growing economy.

Our Departments

Two focused departments delivering excellence in financial and administrative education.

Accounting

Financial accounting, cost accounting, auditing, taxation, and management accounting. Produces certified-ready accounting professionals and educators who understand both theory and real-world practice.

Office & Information Management

Office administration, records management, business communication, information systems, and secretarial studies. Trains efficient administrators and information management specialists.

Career Pathways

COMSED graduates thrive in finance, corporate administration, entrepreneurship, and education.
