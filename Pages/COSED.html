Advancing Scientific Knowledge Through Education

The College of Science Education (COSED) is one of the foremost colleges at LASUED, dedicated to producing skilled educators and researchers in the core sciences. The college combines rigorous academic instruction with practical laboratory experiences to prepare graduates who can teach, innovate, and lead in the scientific community.

COSED offers both B.Sc. and B.Sc.(Ed.) degree programmes, ensuring that graduates are equipped not only with deep subject matter expertise but also with the pedagogical skills required to inspire the next generation of scientists and thinkers.

Our Departments

Five specialised departments offering world-class programmes in Science Education.

Chemistry

Offers B.Sc. Chemistry and B.Sc.(Ed.) Chemistry. Focus on organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry with strong laboratory training.

Physics

Offers B.Sc. Physics and B.Sc.(Ed.) Physics. Studies mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, and modern physics applications.

Natural Science

Offers B.Sc. Biology, B.Sc.(Ed.) Biology, and B.Sc.(Ed.) Integrated Science. Covers botany, zoology, ecology, and life sciences.

Mathematics Education

Offers B.Sc. Mathematics, B.Sc.(Ed.) Mathematics, B.Sc. Statistics, and B.Sc.(Ed.) Statistics. Develops analytical and quantitative skills.

Human Kinetics & Health Education

Offers B.Sc.(Ed.) Human Kinetics and B.Sc.(Ed.) Health Education. Focuses on physical fitness, sports science, and public health promotion.
