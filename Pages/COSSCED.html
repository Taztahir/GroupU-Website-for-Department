Understanding Society to Shape the Future

The College of Social Sciences Education (COSSCED) trains future leaders to understand and shape society through the study of economics, politics, geography, and sociology. Students develop analytical and critical thinking skills essential for governance, policy-making, and social development.

COSSCED produces graduates who are equipped to teach social sciences at all educational levels, while also excelling in banking, government, urban planning, research, and community development.

Our Departments

Four departments covering the core disciplines of social science education.

Economics

Microeconomics, macroeconomics, development economics, and economic policy analysis. Prepares economists and finance professionals.

Geographical & Environmental Education

Physical geography, human geography, environmental studies, GIS, and urban planning. Trains environmentalists and spatial analysts.

Political Science

Government, political theory, public administration, international relations, and civic education. Produces governance experts and policy makers.

Sociological Studies Education

Social structures, cultural studies, demography, criminology, and community development. Develops social researchers and advocates.
