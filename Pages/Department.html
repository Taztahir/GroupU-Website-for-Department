8 Colleges of Excellence

LASUED operates a collegiate system with eight specialized colleges, each designed to produce the next generation of innovative educators, professionals, and industry leaders.

COSED

College of Science Education

Developing expertise in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, and Human Kinetics through advanced pedagogical techniques and hands-on laboratory research.

COHED

College of Humanities Education

Exploring history, diplomatic studies, fine arts, music, theatre arts, and religious studies to cultivate cultural awareness and creative expression.

COITED

COVED

COSSCED

COLCAED

Mastering English, Foreign Languages, Nigerian Languages, and Communication Arts to bridge cultures and excel in media, diplomacy, and education.
