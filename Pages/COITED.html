Powering the Digital Future of Education

The College of Information and Technology Education (COITED) provides a strong foundation in science, technology, and information management. Students gain expertise in computing, educational technology, library sciences, and industrial technology — preparing them for the digital age.

COITED produces graduates who are not only skilled ICT educators but also competent professionals ready for Nigeria's growing tech industry, from software development to automotive engineering.

Our Departments

Four departments covering computing, educational technology, library science, and industrial trades.

Computer Science

Programming, software development, database management, networking, and hardware systems for aspiring IT professionals and educators.

Educational Technology

Application of modern technology to teaching and learning, including e-learning platforms, multimedia design, and instructional technology.

Library & Information Science

Information management, digital archiving, cataloguing, and library administration for the modern information landscape.

Industrial Technology Education

Specialized technical training in Woodwork Technology, Metal Technology, and Automobile Technology for hands-on trade mastery.
