Training Specialised Professionals in Education

The College of Specialised and Professional Education (COSPED) focuses on training professionals in specialised educational fields. From early childhood development to special needs education and school administration, COSPED shapes educators who can serve every learner.

COSPED graduates are uniquely equipped to lead schools, support students with diverse needs, guide career development, and manage educational institutions at every level of the Nigerian education system.

Our Departments

Four departments addressing the full spectrum of professional education needs.

Early Childhood & Primary Education

Child development, nursery and primary school pedagogy, play-based learning, and curriculum design for young learners aged 0–11.

Adult & Special Education

Inclusive education, learning disabilities, sign language, braille, adult literacy programmes, and community education outreach.

Educational Management

School leadership, policy formulation, institutional administration, human resource management, and educational planning.

Guidance & Counselling

Career counselling, mental health support, psychotherapy, student welfare, and rehabilitation services for educational contexts.

Career Pathways

COSPED graduates lead in education administration, counselling, special needs services, and child development.

Early Childhood

  • Nursery / Primary School Teacher
  • Montessori Educator
  • Curriculum Developer (Early Years)
  • Crèche / Daycare Centre Director
  • Child Welfare Officer

Special & Adult Education

  • Special Needs Educator
  • Sign Language Interpreter
  • Adult Literacy Facilitator
  • Rehabilitation Counsellor
  • Inclusive Education Consultant

School Leadership

  • School Principal / Head Teacher
  • Education Board Administrator
  • Policy Planner & Analyst
  • HR Manager (Educational Sector)

Counselling & Welfare

  • School Counsellor
  • Career Development Specialist
  • Mental Health Advocate
  • Human Resources Consultant
  • NGO Youth Programme Coordinator
